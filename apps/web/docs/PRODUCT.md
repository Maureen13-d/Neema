# Neema — product context

## Brand statement

**Neema** (Swahili: grace / blessing) is an offline maternal guardian.  
She watches with you through the night, learns what is normal for *you*, and turns warning signs into **one clear action** — spoken in Swahili — while your data stays on the phone.

One-line pitch: *Neema turns a mother’s 2am warning signs into one clear action, and keeps her data on her phone.*

## Primary user

Pregnant or postpartum mother (or CHP helping her on a shared/smartphone). Voice-first, low literacy friendly, calm until RED.

## Job to be done

Between clinic visits, notice danger early → decide → reach CHP/facility without a dashboard of noise.

## Mode

Operate (guardian app). No marketing landing — entry routes into orientation / consent / home. Orientation is a short trust story.
